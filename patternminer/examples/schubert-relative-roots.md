# Circuit analysis guiding a Schubert representation

This [continuation](../../schubert-structure-constants/research/2026-09-14-richardson-circuits/README.md) carried out exact data generation, training, causal interventions and a mathematical argument. The requested 99% independent accuracy requirement remains unmet. The frozen manuscript and the original circuit discovery and confirmation populations were preserved.

For u,v below w in Bruhat order with length defect one, write A_i=c^w_{s_i u,v} and B_j=c^w_{u,s_jv}. Given A_i>0, the model must choose j with B_j>0. Evaluation requires every positive supplied index to succeed, and requires both members of each difficult pair to succeed. The population names refer to the symmetric groups containing the sampled factors; stable outputs can require a larger ambient symmetric group.

The investigation began with a representation using positive distinguished subexpressions. Its short matched trial did not improve accuracy. A consistency penalty for common fixed-point embeddings reduced residual discrepancies without improving the weakest development population. A subsequent attempt to break duplicated parameter symmetry also showed no benefit. These failures remain in the record.

The more useful change came from circuit analysis. Counterfactual pairs agreed on candidate features but had disjoint valid target supports. Interventions isolated a path from first-block MLP outputs at non-candidate indices through second-block attention keys and values. Neuron selection used one set of factor orbits; a further 18 development orbits were excluded from that selection. On those 44 inputs, 16 selected duplicated neuron pairs reproduced 67.45% of the mean isolated-path effect, compared with 20.14% for activation-magnitude-matched random subsets. These groups had already been used in broader diagnostics, so this was not independent confirmation.

An exact preactivation decomposition identified inverse positions of w as the dominant varying contribution on these examples. Two attention heads partially followed the relation w^{-1}(k+1)=v^{-1}(j+1). This suggested adding coordinates of (xy^{-1})(alpha_j), for the six ordered pairs of distinct permutations x,y among u,v,w. The representation improved minimum development accuracy from 95.65% to 97.67% in its matched comparison. It was supplied by the researcher; the result does not show that the model independently discovered it.

The data loop combined neighboring factor pairs, difficult pairs and geometric screening. All 71 suspected errors retained from the screen were confirmed by complete exact Schubert products. A sampled geometric zero never became a training label without an exact check. New training records brought the corpus to 12,054,685 triples and 47,972 difficult pairs. Audits checked factor-orbit separation and duplicate outputs within the new factor groups.

Further refinement passed all twelve development criteria at 99.52% or better. The fresh independent assessment nevertheless failed: difficult-pair accuracy was 96.54% in the S9 population and 96.59% in S10. Ordinary accuracy exceeded 99.5% in every assessed population. That assessment was frozen as a failure and its populations were subsequently used only for development evaluation, with no assessment rows added to training.

The next matched experiment imposed the mathematical independence of B_j from the supplied index i. It improved the minimum across the expanded fifteen development criteria from 97.23% to 98.00%. An additional continuation exposing the simple-root action coefficients directly to attention reached 98.20%; there was no matched control for that additional training. The final model was reevaluated on the full exposed populations and subjected to the fixed earlier neuron interventions. The numerical reports distinguish those development results from the failed independent assessment.

Separately, the [incidence note](../../schubert-structure-constants/research/2026-09-14-richardson-circuits/INCIDENCE_TRANSLATION_DEGREES.md) derives A_i=e deg_t f(x_i(t)g) and B_i=e deg_t f(gx_i(t)), where f defines the incidence hypersurface and e is the generic incidence degree. The argument uses a birational correspondence obtained by lowering a left descent. Its mathematical status is separate from neural accuracy; the note makes no external priority claim.

This case gives a concrete cycle: test a representation, diagnose a failure, isolate a computational contribution, formulate a mathematical description, change the model, and assess again. It also records a persistent limitation: high development accuracy and concentrated intervention effects have not yet yielded an independently reliable selector or a recovered Schubert multiplication rule.
