# Conditional continuation on the protected circuit populations

This plan is recorded while the new independent accuracy assessment and the original circuit discovery and confirmation populations remain unopened. Execute it only after the selected checkpoint passes every independent accuracy criterion. Freeze its hash throughout the study.

The existing protocol in `2026-09-13-circuit-readiness/CIRCUIT_PROTOCOL.md` remains the basis of the study. `formal_scan.py` reads the preassigned discovery factor pairs, retains the fixed round-robin sample of at most 512 difficult pairs, records gradient times paired activation differences, and directly replaces every head and MLP output. It nominates four attention heads by the largest factor-group mean contrast change on originally correct pairs. Direct measurements for all components supplement the gradient approximation. All directions and the complete cohort are retained in the results.

`formal_confirm.py` freezes those four heads and twenty random sets with the same number of heads in each layer before evaluating the preassigned confirmation cohort. It measures head-output replacement, queries, keys, values, patterns, order biases, ablation, retention, and shuffled sources matched on ambient size and candidate mask. Native, identity, complete-restoration and query/key/bias-versus-pattern identities are checked. Its retained-computation result must not be described as a complete circuit unless the recorded fidelity and accuracy support that claim.

The MLP hypothesis is already selected from development data. On the same confirmation cohort, `formal_path.py` will test the earlier fixed ranking of hidden-unit pairs, with no reselection, through the second-layer keys and values at non-candidate tokens. Its **primary contrast** is the group-mean effect of the fixed top 16 pairs minus the mean effect of the twenty previously fixed magnitude-matched random sets of 16 pairs. The primary interval is a 95% percentile bootstrap over factor orbits; a positive lower endpoint supports a concentrated causal contribution along this path. Top 32/64/all-unit interventions, removal, and input decompositions are secondary descriptions. They do not establish a complete multiplication algorithm.

The head study uses discovery for nomination. The MLP ranking and its controls come from the earlier development analysis and are fixed before either formal population is opened. Neither selection is changed in response to confirmation outcomes. The full confirmation cohort, including model errors, is reported alongside the subset on which both members of each pair were originally answered correctly.

Implementation checks are run on development inputs. `FORMAL_INTERFACE_CHECKS.json` checks all intermediate identity replacements, source restoration, the partial-head interfaces, and native-versus-explicit gradients for every used parameter. These checks pass exactly on the present control checkpoint. Repeat them for the final independently qualified checkpoint before opening the formal populations.

If the independent accuracy assessment fails, these scripts remain unexecuted and the formal populations stay protected. An unexecuted plan is not a completed study.
