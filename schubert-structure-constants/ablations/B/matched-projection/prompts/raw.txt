For homogeneous Schubert structure constants c_{u,v}^w=0 in the complete flag variety of type A, how many flag dimensions must be retained to certify vanishing? Among subsets of the union of the right descents of u and v, consider the smallest number of retained dimensions for which the projected Schubert incidence conditions have empty generic intersection. Construct examples for which this number is large as the rank grows. Propose and prove conjectures about this problem.
